# Project Deliverable 3 — Sprint Progress + Safety Cases

## Objectives

Use the two-week sprint to make a significant advance on your system, and
make the case, with evidence, that you did. Alongside it, analyze how your
system can fail.

By the end of this deliverable, your team will have:

- Made **substantial progress** on your system's functionality since D2.
  What counts as substantial depends on your project.
- Added your **FMECA and three safety cases** to your team repo.
- **Presented** that progress to the class.

Worth **70 points**, one grade for the team: 50 for progress, 10 for the
safety cases, 10 for the presentation.

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Sprint in the UG Commons; the instructor meets with each team | Tue. Nov. 17 and Thu. Nov. 19 |
| Progress page and safety cases in your team repo | **11:59 PM Monday, Nov. 23** |
| Team presentation | In class, **Tuesday, Nov. 24** |

---

## 1. Substantial Progress

A **progress page** in your team repo, `docs/progress/index.md`:

- What your system could do at D2, and what it can do now.
- **Evidence**: a short recording or screenshots of the new functionality
  running, and links to the merged PRs that built it.
- What is still mocked or not started.
- What will be in your Dec. 8 demo, and what you're cutting.

## 2. Safety Cases

Your FMECA and three safety cases in GSN, as described in
[Safety Analysis](safety.md), in your team repo under `docs/safety/`.

## 3. Team Presentation (Tuesday, Nov. 24)

**8 minutes, then 5 minutes of questions**, per team. Make the case that
your team made a significant advance during the sprint:

- Show the new functionality **running**: live if you can, a recording if
  not.
- Walk through **one of your safety cases**: the claim, the evidence, and
  what is still undeveloped.

---

## Using AI

Use AI however you like. **Every deliverable must be vetted by a human on
your team.** Everything you say works is something someone on your team
has seen work. **You are entirely responsible for what you present**,
whether AI helped make it or not.
