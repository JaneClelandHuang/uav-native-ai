# Lesson 2 — Multi-UAV Air Traffic Control

## Lesson Objectives

This week moves from understanding the course infrastructure to building
your first independent service on top of it: a standalone Air Traffic
Control (ATC) system for three simultaneously deployed UAVs. You'll
subscribe to telemetry for all three, monitor 3D separation between them,
and intervene when that separation becomes unsafe.

**Native AI focus — AI-Assisted Programming.** Practice disciplined
AI-assisted development: give Claude/GPT architectural context, specify
the behavior you want, inspect what it proposes before running it, debug
collaboratively, and verify the result. Working code isn't sufficient on
its own — you're responsible for understanding and defending everything
you submit.
