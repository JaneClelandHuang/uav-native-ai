# Lesson 3 — Flight-Log Analysis

## Lesson Objectives

You'll work with real UAV flight logs and reconstruct what happened during
a flight from the operational evidence — position and trajectory,
altitude, flight-mode changes, battery state, GPS quality, health
indicators, and status messages. The goal is an evidence-based account of
what happened, when, and what data supports that conclusion.

**Native AI focus — AI-Assisted Data Analysis.** Use AI as a data-analysis
and forensic-reasoning partner rather than an oracle you hand a log to:
formulate questions, build analysis, inspect evidence, develop and test
hypotheses, and draw conclusions the data actually supports.
